How a pro says it

Each sentence comes from a presentation of your slide. Choose the correct word, then press Check.

I'd like to ___ your attention firstly to BOSS Green.

We say "draw someone's attention to" something.

If you ___ at the third line, you'll see the biggest change.

"Look at" is the action. "See" is the result.

Sell-through rose sharply, ___ 39% to 52%.

Use "from ... to" for the start and end points.

This is largely ___ demand for trainers.

"Due to" + noun. "Because" + subject and verb.

HUGO, ___, fell by six percentage points.

"However" can sit between commas. "Although" needs a clause.

BOSS Black dipped slightly, ___ strong demand for suits.

A contrast: it fell even though demand was strong.

On balance, these results are ___.

"-ing" describes the results. "-ed" describes people.

It's highly ___ that we'll meet our autumn target.

"It's highly likely that". "Probably" goes with the verb.

Verb + "by" + the size of the change: it fell by six points.

Noun + "in" + the thing that changed: a rise in BOSS Orange.

"Compared with" or "compared to" last year. The slide compares AW26 with AW25, so "last season" would mean SS26.
